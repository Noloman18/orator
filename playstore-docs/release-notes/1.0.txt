Version 1.0 (build 1)

What's new in this release:

- Read TXT, Markdown, EPUB, and text-based PDF documents offline
- Import books into private app storage and keep saved reading progress
- Narrate books with the device's installed text-to-speech engine
- Control narration from the reader and playback notification
- Adjust speech rate, pitch, voice, and reading-follow settings
- Browse a local library with document metadata and progress
- Documents and reading data stay on the device
