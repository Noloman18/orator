# Store listing — English (en-US)

## App name

Orator

## Short description

Read and listen to documents offline, and export your books to audio.

## Full description

Orator is a calm, offline-first reader that turns your documents into readable
text, spoken narration, and audio files you can keep.

**Bring your own books.** Import plain text, Markdown, EPUB, and text-based PDF
files through Android's system document picker. Your document is copied into
private app storage — with duplicate detection, text-encoding detection, and a
100 MB per-file limit — and stays on your device with your saved reading
progress.

**A reader built for long sessions.** Adjust text size, line spacing, and theme,
and follow the currently spoken sentence. Move through a table of contents,
chapters, and pages. Orator remembers your reading position and progress, so you
can pick up right where you left off, and shows where you are in every book in
your library.

**Listen to your books.** Orator narrates with the offline voices already
installed on your Android device — no account required. Narration works offline
when an eligible voice is installed.
Pick your voice, set speech rate and pitch, preview a voice, and play, pause,
replay, step sentence by sentence, or rewind and fast-forward five sentences.
Control playback from the reader or from the playback notification.

**Translate and keep reading.** Create a translated text copy in your library,
keeping the original. Orator checks your device and offers only translation
languages with an installed offline TTS voice, for both the source and target.
Read, listen to, or export the translated copy with a matching voice. Missing
language packs download over Wi-Fi first, with an optional mobile-data setting;
once installed, they work offline. Chapter divisions are retained; original
images and page styling are not copied.

Machine translation is powered by Google Translate using on-device models.
Automatic translations can contain errors. Learn more about Cloud Translation:
https://cloud.google.com/translate and Google's attribution requirements:
https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate/attribution.

**Save books as audio.** Export an entire book to a single AAC (.m4a) audio file
right from the reader. Exports run in the background — even if you leave the app
or your device reboots — show live progress, and can be cancelled. Finished files
land in Music/Orator on your device, where you can share them, listen anywhere,
or delete them from the Audio exports screen.

**Keep a thought with the book.** Add a typed note or a private voice note at
the exact place you stopped reading or listening. Open Book Notes to play a
voice note, read a text note, or return to its saved place in the book.

Orator needs no account. Documents, reading progress, settings, and book notes
stay on your device; core reading and narration work offline with installed
voices. The Book Notes screen can show a Google-provided footer ad. Orator does
not provide document or note content to that ad.

**Supported formats and limits.** PDF support extracts selectable text; scanned,
encrypted, or image-only PDFs are not supported. DOCX, RTF, MOBI, Kindle formats,
audiobooks, OCR, and cloud voices are outside the current scope.
