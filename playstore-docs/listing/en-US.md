# Store listing — English (en-US)

## App name

Orator

## Short description

Read and listen to documents offline with installed text-to-speech voices.

## Full description

Orator is a calm, offline-first reader that turns your documents into readable text
and spoken narration.

Bring your own books and documents. Import plain text, Markdown, EPUB, and
text-based PDF files through Android's system document picker, then continue reading
from the saved position in a focused reader designed for long sessions.

With Orator you can:

- Read imported documents with adjustable text size, line height, theme, and reading mode.
- Narrate text using offline-capable voices installed on your Android device.
- Choose the voice, speech rate, and pitch that suit you.
- Play, pause, rewind five sentences, or fast-forward five sentences while reading.
- Control narration from the playback notification, including rewind and fast-forward.
- Follow the currently spoken sentence and see your reading progress.
- Keep your library and imported documents on your device for private, offline use.

Orator does not require an account, ads, or an internet connection. Documents are
parsed locally, and the app does not upload your files, record speech, or generate
permanent audio files.

PDF support extracts selectable text. Scanned, encrypted, or image-only PDFs are not
supported. DOCX, RTF, MOBI, Kindle formats, audiobooks, OCR, and cloud voices are
also outside the current scope.
