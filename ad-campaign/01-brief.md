# Campaign 01 — brief

## Objective

Drive installs of Orator from Google's App campaigns (install goal), growing
first-time users at a target cost per install while the Play listing and ratings
build credibility.

## Core message

> Orator keeps your book uninterrupted: no ad breaks, pop-ups, or banners while
> you read or listen. No account required—just calm, offline-capable listening
> with an installed Android voice.

Everything else (EPUB/PDF/Markdown/TXT import, narration, sentence highlighting,
audio export, and book notes) supports this message. The Book Notes screen may
show a small footer ad; it never covers a page or interrupts narration. Do not
describe Orator as completely ad-free, tracking-free, or internet-free.

## Primary audience

- People who read on mobile: commuters, students, language learners, low-vision
  and accessibility users, podcast lovers who want to read hands-free.
- People frustrated with free apps that push ads, subscriptions, or sign-ups.
- English-speaking markets with capable TTS voices installed (see
  `05-targeting-and-budget.md` for locale list).

## Message angles (ranked)

1. **Uninterrupted reading and listening** — no ad breaks or pop-ups in a book.
2. **No account, local books and notes** — documents and notes stay on-device.
3. **Offline-capable narration** — works offline with an installed Android voice.
4. **Product capability** — turn documents into speech, control the pace, export
   audio, and save book notes.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Cost per install (CPI) | ≤ $1.50 blended after week 2 (see measurement plan) |
| Install rate from store page visits | ≥ 40% (Play conversion tracked by Google) |
| Asset coverage | Five independent headlines/descriptions plus varied images and video at launch |
| Asset report | Review the relative Waiting/Learning/Low/Good/Best ratings after enough delivery |
| Day-30 retention | ≥ 10%, when a retained measurement source is available |
| Crash-free sessions | ≥ 99% in Play Console |

## Launch checklist

1. Confirm Play listing (`playstore-docs/`) is live, uses current screenshots,
   and truthfully discloses the Book Notes footer ad.
2. Set up the campaign per `05-targeting-and-budget.md`.
3. Upload the copy from `02-ad-copy.md` (five headlines + five descriptions).
4. Upload image assets per `04-creative-assets.md` (at least 4).
5. Upload at least one video (scripts in `03-video-scripts.md`).
6. Confirm the automatically created Google Play download conversion. Add a
   meaningful in-app conversion only when its collection and privacy disclosures
   have been implemented and reviewed.
7. Enable auto-applied recommendations only after week 2, and review each one.
