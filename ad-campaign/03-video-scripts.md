# Video scripts

Three scripts, one per message angle. Each is 15-30 seconds and designed to be
produced cheaply: screen recordings of the real app, a calm voice-over, and the
deep teal `#285E61` branding. Google accepts landscape (1920x1080), portrait
(1080x1920), and square (1080x1080); produce each script in landscape first,
then portrait and square crops with safe margins (keep all text inside the
center 60% for portrait to avoid feed overlays).

Use the speech-bubble-free layout: title text top, app UI center, logo bottom.
All on-screen text must also be spoken or duplicated in the description.

## Script 1 — "The promise" (uninterrupted-book angle, ~20s)

The differentiator, straight up.

| Time | Visual | Audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:03 | Deep teal screen. A notification banner slides in: "Now playing: an ad." | Everyone knows this moment. You open an app and you're not using the app. |
| 0:03-0:07 | Phone mockup shows Orator reader, a waveform pulses over highlighted text. | Orator is different. Your book stays uninterrupted. |
| 0:07-0:13 | Screen recording: import EPUB, then text-to-speech starts, sentence highlighted as it is spoken. | Open a PDF, an EPUB, anything. Tap play. Orator reads it aloud with the voice already on your phone. |

| 0:17-0:20 | Orator logo on teal. Voice-over reads the tagline. | Orator. Turn any document into speech. |

## Production notes

- Use the real app for all screen recordings; never fabricate UI.
- Use the Google TTS voice for the narration in recordings, matching what users
  actually hear.
- Keep copy on screen for at least 2 seconds and readable at small sizes.
- First 2-3 seconds must work on mute (clear visual of the app or the
  "No ad breaks while reading" claim on screen).
- Add captions for sound-off viewing.
- Do not show the Book Notes footer ad beside a blanket "no ads" statement.
