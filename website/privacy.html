Privacy, in
plain language.

This policy explains how the TTS Orator Android app handles information. Last updated: September 19, 2026.

The short version

TTS Orator is designed to work with the documents and voices on your device. We do not require an account or upload your books, notes, reading progress, or settings to a Segoo Inc. server.

Who we are

TTS Orator is provided by Segoo Inc. (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, contact us at segoo.inc@gmail.com.

Your documents and notes

When you import a document, TTS Orator copies it into private app storage on your device so it can be read and narrated. Your documents, reading position, display and voice settings, typed notes, and recorded voice notes remain on your device. The app does not send their contents to us.

Audio export

When you choose to export a book, the app creates an AAC audio file on your device, normally in Music/Orator. You control whether to keep, share, or delete that file.

Advertising

TTS Orator may display a Google-provided footer ad in the Book Notes screen. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad-interaction information to provide, measure, and protect advertising, in accordance with Google's policies and your applicable device-level choices.

We do not provide the contents of your documents or notes to the ad. For more information about Google's handling of data in advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

Permissions

Files and media
Used when you choose a document to import and when you save an audio export.
Microphone
Used only when you choose to record a private voice note.
Notifications
Used for playback controls and progress updates while an audio export runs.
Internet
Used for Google-provided advertising and related network functions. Reading and eligible installed voices can work offline.

Children

TTS Orator does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system and does not send the contents of imported documents or notes to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy or TTS Orator, email segoo.inc@gmail.com.
